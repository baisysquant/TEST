`findmnt` offers a highly valuable verification mode (`findmnt --verify`)， to specifically reads the `/etc/fstab` file and performs the following in-depth checks to ensure the configuration is valid

syntax checks (verifying the correct number of columns and checking for missing fields)
device validity (confirming that the corresponding block device, UUID, or LABEL actually exists on the system)
and mount point checks (verifying the existence of the directory and the correctness of its permissions).
